**Comments** let members discuss a page without changing its content.

## Capabilities

- **Create** — anyone with the `commenter` space role (or higher) can comment.
- **Resolve** — mark a thread as handled.
- **Moderate** — remove or manage others' comments.

Authorization is **author-or-permission**: you can always act on your own
comment; acting on someone else's requires the moderate capability. Content-level
comment actions are governed by the **space role**, not organization RBAC.

## Where they live

Comments attach to a page and are shown alongside it. Resolving a comment keeps it
for the record while clearing it from the active view.
