**Comments** let members discuss a page without changing its content.

## Capabilities

- **Create** — anyone with the `commenter` space role (or higher) can comment.
- **Resolve** — mark a thread as handled.
- **Moderate** — remove or manage others' comments.

Authorization is **author-or-permission**: you can always act on your own
comment; acting on someone else's requires the moderate capability. Content-level
comment actions are governed by the **space role**, not organization RBAC.

## Where they live

A comment either belongs to the **page** — shown in the section under the
document — or to a **passage of text**. Both kinds coexist, and replies inherit
the kind of the thread they answer.

### Inline comments

Select text while editing and choose **Kommentieren** (⌘/Ctrl + Alt + M). The
passage is highlighted, and the thread appears in the margin rail beside it,
ordered by its position in the document rather than by age. Clicking a highlight
focuses its thread and vice versa; overlapping passages are normal and each keeps
its own thread. Readers see the highlights and the rail without opening the
editor.

The anchor is stored on the comment as opaque JSON that the API never
interprets. It names a mark inside the document, so concurrent editing moves it
with its text instead of leaving it pointing at a stale offset.

If the annotated text is deleted, the comment is **not** deleted and does not
jump elsewhere: it stays in the rail marked as orphaned, next to the excerpt of
the text it was originally made on.

Resolving a comment keeps it for the record while clearing it from the active
view; resolved inline threads can be shown again from the rail.
